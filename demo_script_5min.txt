SECTION 5 | 02:45 - 03:35 | TRANSFORMATIONS & STAR SCHEMA (WEIGHTAGE: 2.5%)
================================================================================
[SCREEN TO DISPLAY]
Airflow UI -> `dag_03_transform` (or Terminal showing DuckDB SQL models).

[WHAT TO POINT OUT]
- Dual-Engine Architecture: PostgreSQL for staging -> DuckDB 1.1 for columnar OLAP.
- Fact Constellation / Star Schema Layout:
  1. `fact_pharmacy_sale` (Transactional Grain, 1 row = 1 sale item, ~550k rows).
  2. `fact_hospital_demand` (Periodic Monthly Snapshot Grain, 1 row = 1 hospital-medicine-month).
- 9 Conformed & Specialized Dimensions:
  * `dim_medicine` with **SCD Type 2** (tracks wholesale/retail price changes with `IsCurrent`,
    `ValidFromDate`, `ValidToDate`, and surrogate `MedicineKey`).
  * `dim_date` (role-playing calendar dimension for Sale Date, Delivery Date, Expiry Date).
  * `dim_hospital` (with beds banded into Small, Medium, Large `SizeBand`).
  * `dim_pharmacy` (with GLN zero-padding and channel derivation).
  * `dim_sale_profile` & `dim_request_profile` (Junk Dimensions compressing return flags and OTIF status).
- SCD-2 Live Proof: Show how `MED005` has two versions in `dim_medicine` (Version 1 closed, Version 2 current).

[WHAT TO SAY]
"Pillar 4 is Transformation and Dimensional Modeling.
In `dag_03_transform`, we load staged records into DuckDB 1.1. We selected DuckDB for its
vectorized columnar execution engine, allowing multi-table joins and window functions across
a million rows in under 400 milliseconds.

We modeled the Sales & Demand Intelligence (SDI) Mart with two distinct grains:
1. `fact_pharmacy_sale` at the transactional grain with financial metrics like Net Sales, COGS, and Margin.
2. `fact_hospital_demand` at the monthly periodic snapshot grain tracking fulfillment shortfalls.

Our schema features 9 dimensions, including junk dimensions to collapse discrete flags,
role-playing date keys, and conformed dimensions ready for the enterprise constellation.

Crucially, observe `dim_medicine`: it implements Slowly Changing Dimension Type 2.
When Cycle 2 updated prices for MED005, the pipeline expired the original record with
`IsCurrent = 'N'` and created a new current record with a new surrogate key. Prior transactions
remain bound to the historical surrogate key, guaranteeing that historical margin reporting
remains mathematically unaltered."
